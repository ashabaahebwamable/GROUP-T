## Method

1. Choose three items from `knowledge/records/inventory.csv` that are below reorder level.
2. For each, take the quotations available in `knowledge/records/quotations.csv` and the letters in `knowledge/quotations/`.
3. Working on paper, with a calculator, and reading `knowledge/policy/procurement-policy.md` as needed: decide the quantity, compare the quotations, choose a supplier, and write the requisition by hand.
4. Time each case from start to finish. Do not stop the clock to look up policy.
5. Afterwards, check your own work against the computed figures and record every error.
