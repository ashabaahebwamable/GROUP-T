# Procurement Policy (synthetic)

**Version 1.0 — effective 1 July 2026**
Applies to the purchase of goods for site operations.

## 1. Requisition requirement

1.1 No purchase of goods may proceed without an approved purchase requisition.
1.2 A requisition covers one stock item. Requisitions covering several items are not accepted under this version of the policy.
1.3 Every requisition must record the quantity required, the recommended supplier, the comparison on which the recommendation rests, and the name of the officer preparing it.

## 2. Quotation requirement

2.1 A minimum of **three valid quotations** must be obtained for any requisition with a total value of UGX 1,000,000 or above.
2.2 Below UGX 1,000,000, a minimum of **two valid quotations** is required.
2.3 A quotation is valid only if it is within its stated validity period on the date the requisition is prepared. A quotation whose validity period has lapsed must be excluded from comparison.
2.4 A quotation must state the item, the quantity, the unit of measure, the rate, and the treatment of value-added tax. A quotation that does not state its unit of measure is incomplete and must not be used until the supplier clarifies it.

## 3. Comparison basis

3.1 Quotations must be compared on **landed cost**, being the total cost to place the goods on site: the quoted rate, value-added tax where exclusive, and any separately charged delivery or off-loading cost.
3.2 Where suppliers quote in different units, all quotations must be converted to the base unit recorded in the inventory register before comparison.
3.3 The recommended supplier is the valid quotation with the lowest landed cost per base unit, except where clause 4 or clause 5 applies.
3.4 Where two quotations are within two per cent of each other on landed cost, the shorter delivery lead time is preferred.

## 4. Supplier status

4.1 Suppliers marked **excluded** in the supplier register must not be recommended and their quotations must not be counted toward the minimum in clause 2.
4.2 A supplier marked **preferred** carries no price advantage. Preference may be applied only where landed cost is equal.
4.3 Any recommendation of a supplier not on the supplier register requires prior approval by a director.

## 5. Approval thresholds

5.1 Requisitions up to UGX 5,000,000 require approval by the operations manager.
5.2 Requisitions above UGX 5,000,000 require approval by a director.
5.3 No requisition is approved by virtue of its value being small. Every requisition requires an approval decision by a person.
5.4 The officer preparing a requisition may not approve it.

## 6. Overrides

6.1 Any departure from clauses 2, 3 or 4 requires a written reason recorded on the requisition and approval at the level in clause 5.2.
6.2 Overrides are reported monthly to the director.

## 7. Records

7.1 The comparison, the recommendation, the approval decision and any override reason are retained with the requisition.
7.2 Records are retained for six years.
